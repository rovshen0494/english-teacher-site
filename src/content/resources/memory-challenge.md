## Learning Objective

Students recall vocabulary by matching pairs of cards and saying the word aloud when a match is found.

## Materials

8-12 pairs of cards (a picture card and its matching word card, or two versions of the same picture) per small group.

## Preparation

Cut and shuffle the cards, then arrange them face down in a grid for each group of 3-4 students.

## How It Works

1. In small groups, students take turns turning over two cards at a time.
2. If the two cards match, the student says the word aloud and keeps the pair.
3. If they don't match, both cards are turned back face down and it becomes the next student's turn.
4. Play continues until all pairs are found.
5. The student with the most pairs at the end wins that round.

## Student Instructions

Turn over two cards. If they match, say the word and keep them. If not, turn them back and let the next person try.

## Variations

**Easier:** Use fewer pairs (6-8) and allow students to work in pairs rather than individually.

**Harder:** Require a full sentence with the word instead of just saying it when a match is made.

## Extension Activity

At the end, ask each group to sort their collected pairs into two or three categories.

## Teacher Tip

This activity works well for shy students since success depends on memory, not speaking confidence — the speaking happens naturally once a match is made.
