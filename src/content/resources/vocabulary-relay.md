## Learning Objective

Students correctly categorise or recall vocabulary quickly while working as part of a team.

## Materials

A shuffled pile of word cards per team, and two or three labelled categories (e.g. "food," "sports," "jobs").

## Preparation

Prepare one shuffled card pile per team, mixing words from all categories being reviewed.

## How It Works

1. Divide the class into teams and give each team a shuffled pile of word cards face down.
2. Place the category labels at the front of the room or on the board.
3. One student from each team runs up, picks a card, and places it under the correct category.
4. That student runs back and tags the next teammate, who repeats the process.
5. The first team to correctly sort their full pile wins; review any errors together afterward.

## Student Instructions

Pick up a card, decide which category it belongs to, and place it there. Then tag your teammate!

## Variations

**Easier:** Reduce to two clear categories and fewer cards per team.

**Harder:** Add a third category that requires more careful thinking (e.g. "things you can and can't eat").

## Extension Activity

As a class, discuss any cards that caused disagreement about their category and why.

## Teacher Tip

Keep team sizes to 4-6 students so the physical relay portion doesn't create long waiting times between turns.
