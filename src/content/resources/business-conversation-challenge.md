## Learning Objective

Students use appropriate register and functional phrases for common workplace conversations.

## Materials

A set of scenario cards covering situations like requesting a deadline extension, giving an update in a meeting, or making small talk with a colleague.

## Preparation

Choose 3-4 scenarios relevant to your students' actual or likely workplace context.

## How It Works

1. Pre-teach or review key phrases for the scenario type (e.g. polite requests, giving updates, softening disagreement).
2. Pair students and give each pair a scenario card with brief role descriptions.
3. Allow 2-3 minutes of quiet planning time before students begin.
4. Pairs act out the conversation naturally, aiming for a professional but conversational tone.
5. Debrief briefly as a class: which phrases felt useful, and what would you say differently at work?

## Student Instructions

Read your scenario. Have a natural conversation with your partner, using appropriate workplace language.

## Variations

**Easier:** Provide a short phrase bank on the board for reference during the conversation.

**Harder:** Add a complication mid-conversation (e.g. "your colleague pushes back on your request") that students must navigate.

## Extension Activity

Students write a short follow-up email summarising the outcome of their conversation.

## Teacher Tip

Ask students in advance about real situations from their own workplace — using their actual context makes the practice immediately transferable.
