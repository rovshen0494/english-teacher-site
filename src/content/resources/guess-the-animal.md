## Learning Objective

Students form and use simple yes/no questions to identify a hidden animal.

## Materials

A set of animal flashcards covering animals already taught.

## Preparation

Choose one animal card and keep it hidden from the class (in your hand, in an envelope, or on the board turned away).

## How It Works

1. Model the target question forms first, e.g. "Is it big?", "Can it fly?", "Does it live in water?"
2. Tell students you are thinking of one animal from the set they know.
3. Students take turns asking yes/no questions to narrow it down.
4. Answer only "Yes" or "No" to keep students focused on the questions, not on guessing randomly.
5. Once someone guesses correctly, reveal the card and start again with a new hidden animal.

## Student Instructions

Ask questions to find the secret animal. You can only ask "yes or no" questions!

## Variations

**Easier:** Provide a small set of 4-5 possible animals visible on the board to guide question choices.

**Harder:** Remove the visible options entirely so students must think of animals and questions independently.

## Extension Activity

Let a confident student choose the secret animal and answer the class's questions themselves.

## Teacher Tip

Write 3-4 useful question starters on the board before beginning — this gives less confident students a scaffold to build their questions from.
