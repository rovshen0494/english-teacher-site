## Learning Objective

Students speak at length on a range of topics, practising fluency without preparation time.

## Materials

A die for each small group and a matching list of six discussion questions, numbered 1-6.

## Preparation

Write six open discussion questions on a topic relevant to the class (e.g. technology, free time, future plans).

## How It Works

1. Put students into small groups of 3-4 with one die each.
2. The first student rolls the die and answers the matching numbered question for about one minute.
3. The rest of the group listens and can ask one follow-up question.
4. Play passes to the next student, who rolls and answers a new (or repeated) question.
5. Continue until every student has answered at least twice.

## Student Instructions

Roll the die. Answer the matching question for about one minute. Then let your group ask you one question.

## Variations

**Easier:** Reduce speaking time to 30 seconds and simplify the questions.

**Harder:** Require the listener to summarise what the speaker said before asking their follow-up question.

## Extension Activity

As a class, share the most interesting answer each group heard.

## Teacher Tip

Rotating which question comes up keeps the activity fresh — students can't simply prepare one answer in advance and repeat it.
