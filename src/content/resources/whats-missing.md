## Learning Objective

Students recall and say recently learned vocabulary words from memory.

## Materials

5-6 flashcards or small objects representing vocabulary already introduced, plus something to cover them briefly.

## Preparation

Lay out the flashcards or objects in a row where all students can see them.

## How It Works

1. Say each item's name together as a class, pointing to it.
2. Ask students to close their eyes or turn around.
3. Remove one item and hide it.
4. Students open their eyes and try to say which item is missing.
5. Reveal the missing item to confirm, then repeat with a different item.

## Student Instructions

Look carefully, then close your eyes. When you look again, say what is missing!

## Variations

**Easier:** Use only 3-4 items and give a choice of two possible answers.

**Harder:** Remove two items at once for older or more confident students.

## Extension Activity

Once a student guesses correctly, let them choose which item to hide next.

## Teacher Tip

Keep the same 5-6 items across a few lessons so the vocabulary gets genuine repeated practice, not just one exposure.
