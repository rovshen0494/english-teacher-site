## Learning Objective

Students paraphrase and describe vocabulary using alternative words and structures when the obvious words are unavailable.

## Materials

A set of cards, each with a target word and 3-4 "banned" words closely related to it (e.g. target: "school," banned: teacher, student, classroom, learn).

## Preparation

Prepare 15-20 cards using recently taught vocabulary, split into two team decks if playing with two teams.

## How It Works

1. Split the class into two teams and explain the rules: one student describes the target word without saying it or any banned word.
2. Set a timer (30-60 seconds) for each turn.
3. The describer's team shouts guesses until they get the word right or time runs out.
4. If the describer accidentally says a banned word, that card is skipped.
5. Rotate the describer role between turns and keep a running score of correct guesses per team.

## Student Instructions

Describe the word on your card without saying the word itself or any of the banned words. Your team has to guess!

## Variations

**Easier:** Remove the timer and allow describers to use gestures as well as words.

**Harder:** Reduce the time limit and add more banned words per card.

## Extension Activity

After playing, ask students to write their own Taboo card for a new word to add to the deck.

## Teacher Tip

Pre-teach or review the target vocabulary before playing — Taboo works best as practice for known words, not as a way to introduce brand-new ones.
