## Learning Objective

Students ask and answer questions to exchange information neither partner has alone, completing a shared task.

## Materials

Two versions of the same worksheet (e.g. a schedule, map, or picture) each missing different pieces of information.

## Preparation

Print or prepare Worksheet A for half the class and Worksheet B for the other half.

## How It Works

1. Pair students so one has Worksheet A and the other has Worksheet B.
2. Explain that neither worksheet is complete alone — they must ask each other questions to fill in the gaps.
3. Model one or two example questions based on the worksheet type (e.g. "What time does the train leave?").
4. Students take turns asking and answering until both worksheets are complete.
5. Pairs compare their completed worksheets with the answer key to check accuracy.

## Student Instructions

You and your partner have different information. Ask questions to fill in what's missing on your worksheet.

## Variations

**Easier:** Provide a list of question starters alongside the worksheet.

**Harder:** Remove visual support entirely and base the gap on a short listening or reading text instead.

## Extension Activity

Pairs write one additional question about the completed information to ask another pair.

## Teacher Tip

Circulate while pairs work and note common question errors to review briefly with the whole class at the end.
