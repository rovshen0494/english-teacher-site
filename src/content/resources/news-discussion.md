## Learning Objective

Students read for general and specific understanding, then discuss opinions and reactions using appropriate language.

## Materials

A short, level-appropriate news summary (3-4 paragraphs) on a topic likely to interest the class.

## Preparation

Select or adapt an article to match the class's reading level, and prepare 3-4 discussion questions in advance.

## How It Works

1. Pre-teach 4-6 key vocabulary items likely to be unfamiliar before reading.
2. Give students time to read the summary individually or in pairs.
3. Check general understanding with one or two simple comprehension questions.
4. Move into small-group discussion using prepared questions (e.g. "Do you agree with this decision? Why?").
5. Bring groups together to share key points from their discussion.

## Student Instructions

Read the summary. Then discuss your reaction and opinions with your group.

## Variations

**Easier:** Simplify the article further or provide a shorter summary with supporting visuals.

**Harder:** Ask students to find and summarise a related short article themselves before the lesson.

## Extension Activity

Students write a short opinion paragraph responding to the article's topic.

## Teacher Tip

Choose genuinely current, relevant topics where possible — adult learners engage far more with real, timely material than with generic textbook topics.
