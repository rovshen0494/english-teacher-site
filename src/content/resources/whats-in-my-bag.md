## Learning Objective

Students describe simple physical properties (shape, size, texture) to guess a hidden object.

## Materials

An opaque bag or box and 5-8 small, safe objects familiar to students (e.g. pencil, ball, spoon, toy car).

## Preparation

Place the objects inside the bag without students seeing them.

## How It Works

1. Teach or review basic describing words: big, small, round, soft, hard, long.
2. Invite one student to reach into the bag, feel one object without looking, and describe it using the target words.
3. The rest of the class guesses what the object is based on the description.
4. Reveal the object to check, then invite the next student to take a turn.
5. Continue until every student who wants a turn has had one.

## Student Instructions

Feel the object. Don't look! Describe it. Can your classmates guess what it is?

## Variations

**Easier:** Allow students to say single describing words rather than full sentences.

**Harder:** Require a full sentence, e.g. "It is small and round and hard."

## Extension Activity

After all objects are revealed, ask students to sort them into groups by shape or size.

## Teacher Tip

Choose objects with clearly different textures and shapes — this makes the describing language meaningful rather than guesswork.
