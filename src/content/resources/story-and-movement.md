## Learning Objective

Students connect spoken English to meaning by acting out actions and vocabulary as a story is read aloud.

## Materials

A short picture story with repeated, simple language and one or two actions that repeat throughout (e.g. walking, jumping, an animal sound).

## Preparation

Read through the story once beforehand and decide on 2-3 simple actions to attach to repeated words or phrases.

## How It Works

1. Show the cover and ask students to guess what the story might be about, using gestures to support understanding.
2. Read the story slowly and clearly, pausing at the chosen repeated words or phrases.
3. Model the action for that word the first time it appears, and invite students to copy you.
4. Each time the word or phrase reappears, pause and let students do the action before continuing.
5. After the story, ask simple yes/no or one-word questions about what happened.

## Student Instructions

Listen to the story. When you hear the special word, do the action!

## Variations

**Easier:** Reduce to a single repeated action for the whole story.

**Harder:** Add a second action tied to a different repeated phrase, and ask students to alternate between them.

## Extension Activity

Retell the story very briefly using only the actions, and see if students can say the matching words.

## Teacher Tip

Choose a story you can read with genuine expression and varied pace — your delivery does as much work as the words themselves at this age.
