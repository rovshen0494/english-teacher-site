## Learning Objective

Students practise new vocabulary by finding words around the room and using them in a spoken sentence.

## Materials

One set of vocabulary cards (word or picture) covering the current topic, plus tape to stick them up before class.

## Preparation

Hide the vocabulary cards around the classroom — under chairs, on walls, behind objects. Takes about 5 minutes.

## How It Works

1. Review the target vocabulary as a class before starting, so every word is familiar.
2. Explain that students will search the room to find as many cards as they can in a set time.
3. Give a clear time limit (e.g. 5 minutes) and set students searching.
4. When time is up, bring the class back together and go through the words one at a time.
5. For each word a student found, ask them to say a simple sentence using it (e.g. "I found 'dog'. I have a dog.").

## Student Instructions

Find the cards around the room. When you find one, keep it safe. At the end, you will say a sentence with your word.

## Variations

**Easier:** Reduce the number of hidden cards and allow students to work in pairs.

**Harder:** Require a full sentence with a specific structure (e.g. using "There is/are") rather than any sentence.

## Extension Activity

Have students sort their found words into categories (e.g. animals vs. food) once the hunt is finished.

## Teacher Tip

Set clear boundaries for where students can search before starting — this avoids chaos and keeps the activity focused on the language, not just the chase.
