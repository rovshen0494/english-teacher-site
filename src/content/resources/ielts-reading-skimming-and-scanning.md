## Explanation

With only about 20 minutes per passage, IELTS Reading rewards students who read strategically rather than word-for-word. Skimming (reading quickly for general idea and structure) helps locate where information probably is; scanning (searching quickly for specific words, numbers or names) helps answer detail questions fast. The key skill is knowing which technique fits which question type.

## Examples

- For "What is the main purpose of paragraph C?" — skim the paragraph for its general topic; don't read every word.
- For "In what year did the study take place?" — scan for a number or date; ignore surrounding sentences you don't need.
- For matching headings — skim each paragraph's first and last sentence before reading in full.

## Practice Activity

Give the student a short passage and two question types: one general/main-idea question and one specific detail question. Time them: 90 seconds to skim and answer the general question, then 60 seconds to scan and answer the detail question. Review together whether the correct technique was used for each.

## Teacher Tip

Many students try to read every word of every passage the same way. Explicitly naming which technique to use for each question type — and timing the practice — breaks that habit faster than general advice to "read faster."
