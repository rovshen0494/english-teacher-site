## Learning Objective

Students recognise and respond physically to basic colour words in English.

## Materials

Coloured paper squares placed around the room, or classroom objects in different colours. Colour flashcards are optional but useful for pre-teaching.

## Preparation

Place 4-6 different coloured objects or paper squares around the room before the lesson. Takes about 5 minutes.

## How It Works

1. Pre-teach the colour words using flashcards or objects, repeating each word chorally.
2. Explain the game with a demonstration: when you say a colour, students run and touch something that colour.
3. Call out one colour at a time in a clear, energetic voice.
4. Give students a few seconds to find and touch the colour before calling the next one.
5. Increase the pace gradually as students become more confident.

## Student Instructions

Listen for the colour. Run and touch something that colour as fast as you can!

## Variations

**Easier:** Point to the colours as you say them the first few rounds, and slow down the pace.

**Harder:** Call two colours in a row ("Touch something red, then something blue") for older or more confident students in the group.

## Extension Activity

Ask individual students "What colour is this?" while pointing to objects, encouraging a one-word spoken answer.

## Teacher Tip

Keep your voice upbeat and use big gestures — at this age, energy and clarity matter more than long instructions.
