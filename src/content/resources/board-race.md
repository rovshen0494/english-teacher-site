## Learning Objective

Students recall and spell vocabulary quickly within a category under light time pressure.

## Materials

A board or large sheet of paper split into two or more sections, and one marker per team.

## Preparation

None needed beyond splitting the board into team sections.

## How It Works

1. Divide the class into two or more teams and line them up facing the board.
2. Announce a category (e.g. "animals," "food," "sports").
3. On "Go," the first student from each team runs to the board and writes one correct word, then passes the marker to the next teammate.
4. Continue in relay fashion for a set time (e.g. 60-90 seconds).
5. Count correct words per team at the end — misspelled or repeated words don't count.

## Student Instructions

When it's your turn, run to the board and write one word from the category. Then give the marker to your teammate.

## Variations

**Easier:** Allow teammates to whisper suggestions to whoever is writing.

**Harder:** Require full sentences using each word instead of single words.

## Extension Activity

As a class, go through the words on the board together, checking spelling and discussing any unfamiliar ones.

## Teacher Tip

Keep teams small (4-6 students) so everyone gets multiple turns — a class of 30 works well split into five or six teams.
