## Learning Objective

Students construct a coherent narrative collaboratively, using connecting words and reacting to what classmates say.

## Materials

A short story starter sentence for each group (e.g. "One morning, Anna found a strange box in her garden...").

## Preparation

Write or print 4-6 different story starters, one for each group of 4-5 students.

## How It Works

1. Put students into small groups and give each group a story starter sentence.
2. Explain that students will add to the story one sentence at a time, going around the group in order.
3. Encourage the use of connecting words: "Then... After that... Suddenly... Finally..."
4. Let each round continue for 6-8 sentences per group.
5. Each group shares their finished story with the class, either by reading it aloud together or through one narrator.

## Student Instructions

Listen to the story so far, then add one new sentence. Try to make it make sense with what came before!

## Variations

**Easier:** Allow students to discuss their sentence with a partner before saying it aloud.

**Harder:** Add a rule requiring each new sentence to include a specific grammar structure (e.g. past continuous).

## Extension Activity

Groups write their finished story down and illustrate one key moment from it.

## Teacher Tip

Model the first two sentences yourself so students hear the expected sentence length and connecting language before starting.
