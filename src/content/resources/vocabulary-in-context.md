## Learning Objective

Students infer meaning from context and use new vocabulary accurately in their own sentences.

## Materials

A short text or a set of example sentences, each naturally using one of 6-8 target vocabulary words.

## Preparation

Choose or write a short text relevant to the class's interests that naturally includes the target vocabulary.

## How It Works

1. Students read the text and try to guess the meaning of each highlighted word from context, without a dictionary.
2. Check guesses as a class, confirming or clarifying meaning together.
3. Discuss collocations and typical use for each word (e.g. what usually goes with it, formal or informal).
4. Students write their own original sentence for each word, related to their own life or work.
5. In pairs, students read their sentences aloud and check each other's usage.

## Student Instructions

Guess the meaning of each word from the text first. Then write your own sentence using each word correctly.

## Variations

**Easier:** Provide multiple-choice meaning options alongside each word.

**Harder:** Require students to use two target words together in a single sentence.

## Extension Activity

Students test a partner by reading their sentence aloud with the target word replaced by "blank," and the partner guesses the word.

## Teacher Tip

Teaching vocabulary through context rather than translated word lists builds a stronger, more flexible sense of how a word is actually used.
