## Learning Objective

Students review recently studied vocabulary and grammar under quiz conditions, reinforcing recall.

## Materials

A prepared list of 15-20 questions based on recent lesson content, and a way for each team to record answers.

## Preparation

Write questions covering a mix of vocabulary, grammar and general topic knowledge from the current unit.

## How It Works

1. Split the class into small teams of 3-4 students.
2. Read each question aloud (or display it), giving teams a short time to discuss and write their answer.
3. Collect answers after each question, or hold a full round and mark at the end.
4. Reveal the correct answer and award a point for each correct team response.
5. Total the scores at the end and briefly review any question most teams got wrong.

## Student Instructions

Discuss with your team and agree on one answer. Write it down before time runs out.

## Variations

**Easier:** Provide multiple-choice options for each question.

**Harder:** Add a time-pressure round where the first correct team to raise their answer gets bonus points.

## Extension Activity

Teams write one quiz question of their own to add to a future review quiz.

## Teacher Tip

Mix easier and harder questions throughout, rather than grouping by difficulty — this keeps every team engaged from the first question to the last.
