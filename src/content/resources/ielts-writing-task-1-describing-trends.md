## Explanation

Task 1 asks candidates to summarise visual information — usually a graph, chart or table — in at least 150 words. The most common mistake is describing every single data point rather than identifying the overall trend and selecting the most significant features to support it. A strong Task 1 response has four parts: an introduction (paraphrasing the question), an overview of the main trends, and two body paragraphs with supporting detail and figures.

## Examples

Overview sentence for a graph showing rising internet use: "Overall, internet usage increased steadily across all three countries over the period shown, with the most significant growth occurring in Country A."

Useful trend language: *rose steadily, increased sharply, remained stable, fluctuated, declined gradually, peaked at, reached a low of.*

## Practice Activity

Give the student a simple line graph with two or three data series. Ask them to write only the overview paragraph first (2-3 sentences identifying the main trend, without specific numbers). Check it identifies the single most important pattern before moving on to a full response with supporting data.

## Teacher Tip

Many students skip the overview or bury it in the middle of the response. Teach it as a separate, non-negotiable paragraph — examiners specifically look for it, and its absence caps the score regardless of how good the rest of the writing is.
