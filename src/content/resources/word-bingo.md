## Learning Objective

Students recognise target vocabulary by ear and match it to written words on their bingo grid.

## Materials

A 3x3 or 4x4 grid for each student with target vocabulary words already written in, plus small items to use as markers.

## Preparation

Prepare or print grids with 9-16 words from the current topic, arranged in a different order on each grid.

## How It Works

1. Give each student a grid and a small pile of markers.
2. Call out words from the topic list one at a time, in random order.
3. Students place a marker on the word if it appears on their grid.
4. The first student to complete a line (or the full grid) calls "Bingo!"
5. Check their grid together by having them say each covered word aloud.

## Student Instructions

Listen carefully. If you hear a word on your grid, cover it. Shout "Bingo!" when you get a full line.

## Variations

**Easier:** Use fewer words (6-9) and repeat each word twice before moving on.

**Harder:** Instead of saying the word, give a short definition or description and have students find the matching word.

## Extension Activity

Once a student wins, have them read out their full winning line so the class hears the words again.

## Teacher Tip

Keep a checklist of which words you have already called — it makes verifying a winning grid quick and avoids disputes.
