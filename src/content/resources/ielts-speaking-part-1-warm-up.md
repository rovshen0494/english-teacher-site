## Explanation

IELTS Speaking Part 1 asks familiar, personal questions about topics like home, work, studies and daily routine. The challenge is rarely the topic itself — it's that many candidates answer in one short sentence when the examiner is listening for range: varied vocabulary, natural extension, and accurate everyday grammar. A strong Part 1 answer gives a direct response and then adds one extra piece of information or a brief reason.

## Examples

**Question:** Do you work or are you a student?

Weak answer: "I'm a student."

Stronger answer: "I'm a student — I'm in my final year of a business degree, so I'm quite busy with exams at the moment."

**Question:** What do you usually do in your free time?

Stronger answer: "I mostly relax by cooking, actually. I find it's a good way to switch off after a long day."

## Practice Activity

Give the student five Part 1 questions from different topics (home, work/study, hobbies, food, daily routine). For each one, the student answers in under 20 seconds using the pattern: direct answer + one extra detail or reason. Record or note the answers, then review together, checking whether each answer genuinely extended beyond the first sentence.

## Teacher Tip

Time each answer. If a student consistently finishes in under 10 seconds, they are very likely under-extending — gently prompt with "Can you tell me a bit more about that?" until the habit of adding detail becomes automatic.
