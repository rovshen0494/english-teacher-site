## Learning Objective

Students practise introductions and small talk fluently across multiple short conversations.

## Materials

A short list of prompt questions (e.g. "What do you do?", "What did you do last weekend?") and a way to time rounds.

## Preparation

Arrange the room into two facing rows or a circle so students can pair up quickly and rotate.

## How It Works

1. Explain the format: students will have several short conversations, moving to a new partner each round.
2. Give a conversation prompt or let students choose their own opening question.
3. Set a timer for 2-3 minutes per round.
4. When time is up, one row moves down one seat (or students find a new partner) and a new round begins.
5. Repeat for 4-5 rounds, optionally changing the prompt each round.

## Student Instructions

Talk to your partner until time is up. When the timer rings, move to a new partner and start again.

## Variations

**Easier:** Provide the same prompt for every round so students can build confidence with repetition.

**Harder:** Require students to ask at least one follow-up question before the round ends.

## Extension Activity

After the activity, ask students to share one interesting thing they learned about a classmate.

## Teacher Tip

Repetition across rounds is a feature, not a flaw — adult learners often become noticeably more fluent by their third or fourth conversation.
