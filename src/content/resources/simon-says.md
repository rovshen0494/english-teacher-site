## Learning Objective

Students listen carefully and respond correctly to simple instructions using action verbs.

## Materials

None — this activity needs no preparation or materials.

## Preparation

No preparation needed.

## How It Works

1. Teach or review a small set of action verbs (stand up, sit down, clap, jump, turn around).
2. Explain that students should only follow the instruction if it starts with "Simon says."
3. Give an instruction, e.g. "Simon says clap your hands," and do the action with them at first.
4. Occasionally give an instruction without "Simon says" — students who follow it are out for that round, or simply reset and continue with younger groups.
5. Speed up gradually and stop modelling the actions once students are confident.

## Student Instructions

Only do the action if the teacher says "Simon says" first. Listen carefully!

## Variations

**Easier:** Keep everyone in the game the whole time (no eliminations) and continue modelling actions throughout.

**Harder:** Add two-step instructions, e.g. "Simon says touch your toes and then clap."

## Extension Activity

Let a confident student take a turn being "Simon" and giving instructions to the class.

## Teacher Tip

With very young learners, skip the elimination rule entirely — the goal is active listening and fun, not a strict competition.
