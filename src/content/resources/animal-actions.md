## Learning Objective

Students say and recognise animal names and connect each word to a physical action.

## Materials

Animal flashcards are helpful but not essential — pictures can also be drawn quickly on the board.

## Preparation

Choose 4-6 animals with clear, easy actions (monkey, elephant, bird, fish, snake, frog).

## How It Works

1. Introduce each animal one at a time with its flashcard or picture, saying the word clearly.
2. Model a simple action for each animal (e.g. flap arms for bird, wiggle for snake) and have students copy you.
3. Say an animal name and have the whole class do the action together.
4. Once familiar, hold up the flashcard silently and have students say the animal name before doing the action.
5. Mix the order and speed up to keep energy high.

## Student Instructions

Watch, listen, and become the animal! Say the name if you can.

## Variations

**Easier:** Do the action together as a class every time, with no individual turns.

**Harder:** Call on individual students to do the action alone while the rest of the class guesses and says the animal name.

## Extension Activity

Play a short "freeze" round: students move like the animal until you say "Freeze!", then hold the position.

## Teacher Tip

Choose animals with distinct, easy-to-copy movements — this keeps transitions between animals quick and clear.
