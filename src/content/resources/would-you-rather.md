## Learning Objective

Students state a preference and give a simple reason for it, using basic opinion language.

## Materials

A set of "Would you rather...?" cards with two contrasting options (e.g. "Would you rather live in the city or the countryside?").

## Preparation

Prepare or print 10-15 question cards appropriate to the class's age and interests.

## How It Works

1. Model the format with one example, giving your own answer and a short reason.
2. Read a "Would you rather" question aloud to the class.
3. Students move to one side of the room or raise a hand to show their choice.
4. In pairs or small groups, students explain their choice to each other using "I would rather... because..."
5. Ask a few volunteers to share their answer and reason with the whole class before moving to the next question.

## Student Instructions

Choose your answer, then explain why using "I would rather... because..."

## Variations

**Easier:** Allow a one-word or short-phrase reason instead of a full sentence.

**Harder:** Require students to disagree politely with a partner's choice and explain why they'd choose differently.

## Extension Activity

Students write their own "Would you rather" question to ask a partner.

## Teacher Tip

Choose questions genuinely relevant to your students' age and culture — locally relevant topics get much richer responses than generic ones.
