## Learning Objective

Students use appropriate functional language for a real-life situation, adapting their register to the scenario.

## Materials

Paired role cards describing a scenario and each student's role within it (e.g. customer and shop assistant, tourist and hotel receptionist).

## Preparation

Choose 3-4 scenarios relevant to students' likely real-world needs and prepare matching role cards.

## How It Works

1. Pre-teach or review key functional phrases needed for the scenario (e.g. asking for a refund, checking into a hotel).
2. Put students in pairs and give each pair a scenario with two role cards.
3. Give a few minutes for students to read their role and think about what they need to say.
4. Pairs act out the conversation, staying in role throughout.
5. Ask one or two pairs to perform their role play for the class, then briefly discuss language choices.

## Student Instructions

Read your role card. Act out the conversation with your partner, using natural English for the situation.

## Variations

**Easier:** Provide a short list of useful phrases on the board for reference during the role play.

**Harder:** Add an unexpected complication to the scenario partway through (e.g. "the item is out of stock") that students must respond to.

## Extension Activity

Pairs swap roles and repeat the same scenario from the other person's perspective.

## Teacher Tip

Circulate during the role play and note useful language or common errors to highlight in the whole-class discussion afterward, rather than interrupting mid-conversation.
